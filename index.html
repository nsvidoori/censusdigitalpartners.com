Focused on Decennial 2030

Based on 2020 success and ongoing innovations.

  • 2030 Solutions Integration Research Services (SIRS)

    Built a customized Architecture Development Methodology (ADM) framework for 2030 statistical systems; PB-scale data platforms bring AI/ML to Federal analytics.

  • 2026 Census Test IT Integration Support

    Developed an application environment management framework, including critical design review, environment and infrastructure design specifications, and process validation for EDL-delivered systems.

  • USCB Enterprise Testing Support Services (ETest)

    Centralized support for enterprise-level testing throughout USCB.

  • USCB Clerical Matching and Map Update System (CMMU)

    Research support for new technologies and data science solutions to redesign and re-engineer the 2030 Census Post-Enumeration Survey Clerical Matching and Map Update (PES CMMU) system.

  • USCB 2026 Census Test Application Development (CTAD)

    Transforming USCB technologies into data-centric, modern, cloud-based IT solutions—modernizing existing applications and developing new ones to support the Decennial Census.
